Veterinary Ophthalmology Research Agent

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 18, 2026

Scope

This policy explains how Veterinary Ophthalmology Research Agent (the “Application”), a private research tool operated by its owner, accesses and handles Google user data.

Google user data accessed

The Application uses the Gmail read-only permission. It searches for messages from NCBI or NIH that contain PubMed or NCBI literature alerts and reads the subject and message content needed to extract PubMed identifiers.

How the data is used

Google user data is used solely to identify PubMed literature alerts and add the associated PubMed identifiers to the owner's veterinary ophthalmology research workflow. The Application does not use Gmail data for advertising, user profiling, or unrelated purposes.

Storage and retention

Processing occurs locally on the owner's computer. The Application may retain the Gmail message identifier, message subject, received and processing timestamps, and extracted PubMed identifiers in a local database. The raw email body is not retained. The OAuth credential is stored locally and is not published with this website.

Sharing and disclosure

The Application does not sell Google user data and does not share Gmail message content or OAuth credentials with third parties. Extracted PubMed identifiers may be used to retrieve public scientific metadata from literature services as part of the research workflow.

Protection

Access is limited to the owner-controlled environment. The Application requests only read-only Gmail access and cannot send, delete, modify, label, archive, or mark messages as read.

Deletion and revocation

The owner can delete locally retained records and OAuth credentials from the local application environment. Google account access can also be revoked at any time from the Google Account third-party connections page.

Contact

Questions about this policy may be submitted through the public repository issue tracker. Do not include personal, confidential, or Gmail message data in a public issue.
